01 — About

Systems thinking, applied to business.

I studied computer science, but my career has lived at the join between technology and operations — the place where a business process becomes a system, and a system becomes somebody's daily work. That's the seat I take on every ERP engagement.

Since 2025 I've worked inside live Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central and ERPNext implementations, carrying requirements from first conversation to BRD/FRD, through UAT and Go-Live, into Hypercare and steady-state support. I've governed master data at volume, reconciled revenue across ERP and POS, and kept identity and access under control across branches and roles — because an ERP is only as trustworthy as the data feeding it.

I bring the same instinct from IT operations: hardware, networks, Microsoft 365, Entra ID. Every ticket resolved and every access review completed feeds the same goal — systems people can rely on without thinking about them.

Work Philosophy

  • Data first. No configuration is trustworthy on top of dirty master data.
  • Document the AS-IS before designing the TO-BE. Assumptions are expensive at Go-Live.
  • Support doesn't end at Go-Live. Hypercare is where trust in the system is actually earned.
